LOCAL PROPERTY GUIDE

What to check before buying property in Varanasi

Last reviewed: September 20, 2026. This practical checklist is for general information and is not a substitute for legal advice.

  1. Ask the seller for the sale deed and earlier title documents available for the property.
  2. Confirm that the seller's identity and ownership details match the documents.
  3. Ask about mortgages, claims, disputes, or other encumbrances and request supporting records where applicable.
  4. For plots or construction, check the relevant land-use, approval, access, and development documents for that property.
  5. Before paying a substantial advance, have a qualified local property lawyer review the documents and transaction terms.

Mishra Property Dealer can help coordinate property enquiries, site visits, document collection, and registry-related paperwork. Legal title opinions should be obtained from an independent qualified professional.
