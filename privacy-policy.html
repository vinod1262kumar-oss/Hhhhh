Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What information we collect

When you send an enquiry through this website — on the contact page or from a specific property — we collect the name, phone number, and any message you choose to provide. We also record which page you visited (for basic analytics, such as which pages get the most views), without collecting any information that identifies you personally beyond what you submit in an enquiry.

How we use it

Information submitted through an enquiry form is used only to respond to that enquiry — typically by phone, WhatsApp, or in person. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and it is not used for marketing you did not ask for.

How it's stored

Enquiries and basic page-view analytics are stored securely using our hosting provider's infrastructure. We keep this information only as long as it's useful for following up with you, and we take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

Your choices

If you'd like your information removed from our records, contact us using the details on our Contact page and we'll action it.
